S&P Central: Melodies

The complete Melodies directory of S&P Central — some six hundred recordings of the Western Sephardi tradition, gathered from Amsterdam, London, New York, Bordeaux, Florence, Gibraltar, Rome, and beyond.

The Esnoga (Portuguese Synagogue), Amsterdam.

About this archive

This index was compiled by the Spanish & Portuguese community at S&P Central (sandpcentral.org); its Melodies directory is gathered here in full, with gratitude to its makers.

Every entry links out to its original home — YouTube, SoundCloud, and the cantorial archives of Amsterdam, London, New York, Bordeaux, Florence, and others — which remain online; the small grey label on each shows where it leads. These are not recordings of our Kehillah: they are the wider tradition from which our melodies are drawn, gathered here for study and for the love of the song.
